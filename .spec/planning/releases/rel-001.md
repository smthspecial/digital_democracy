## Goal

Deliver one secure, browser-accessible governance journey: verified citizen,
scope-derived eligibility, informed ballot, certified result, and publicly
tracked project.

## Exit Criteria

- Citizen identity comes from a validated bearer session, never a caller-supplied header or body field.
- Eligibility tokens are issued from verified residency and jurisdiction membership.
- Voting policy and package are derived from proposal scope and required information.
- A certified successful vote creates one project idempotently.
- The journey runs against both applications, Postgres, and NATS in CI.
