## Description

As a citizen, I can cast my ballot with fast, consistent response times even when hundreds of thousands of other citizens are voting at the same time, so that election-day load never becomes a barrier to participation.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The system sustains 100,000 concurrent ballot-casting sessions with ballot:cast p99 latency of 1 second or less
- [ ] A regional infrastructure outage does not prevent citizens in other regions from casting ballots
- [ ] A ballot is durably written before the citizen sees a confirmation, with no silent data loss under load
