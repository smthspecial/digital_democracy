## Description

As the system, I can lose an entire data-center region and keep operating so that no single infrastructure failure can disrupt national governance.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Automated failover completes within 5 minutes for governance-critical data (ballot, audit_log, governance_role)
- [ ] No cast ballot or audit log entry is lost across a regional failover (recovery point objective of 30 seconds or less)
- [ ] Regional failover is tested in a full drill at least once per quarter, with results published

## Notes

- Realizes: NFR-011
- Technical aspects: ADR-017, ARCH-008
- No region failure stops governance; no vote or audit record is lost.
