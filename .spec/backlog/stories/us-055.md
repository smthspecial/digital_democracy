## Description

As an on-call platform-operator, I can request emergency elevated access during a declared incident so that I can respond quickly, while the grant remains temporary, co-approved, and reviewed after the fact.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Break-glass access requires real-time co-approval from a second independent platform-operator
- [ ] Break-glass access auto-expires after 4 hours or less even if never manually revoked
- [ ] Every break-glass grant automatically triggers a mandatory post-incident review by an active audit body within 48 hours
