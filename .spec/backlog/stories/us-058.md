## Description

As an auditor, I can review published load-test results and disaster-recovery drill outcomes so that claims about the system's scale and reliability are independently verifiable rather than taken on trust.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Load-test results are published before and after each national vote session
- [ ] Disaster-recovery drill results are published on a quarterly cadence
- [ ] Both are readable by any citizen through the public audit log, not restricted to internal staff

## Notes

- Realizes: NFR-009, NFR-011, FR-066
- Technical aspects: ARCH-007, ARCH-008
- Scale and reliability claims stay verifiable, not just asserted.
