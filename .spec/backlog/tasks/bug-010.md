## Finding

Severity: **High**. Assessment: **SEC-06**.

An expired-deadline session was opened and accepted a ballot. Option inserts succeeded after opening and after certification in both stores. The handlers check stored state/existence but do not enforce these integrity constraints.

## Evidence

See [security report](../../../testing/security/REPORT.md) and its sanitized
memory/Postgres result files. Source locations, fixtures and scope limits are
recorded there. Run [TP-016](../../technical/test-plans/tp-016.md) for reproduction.

## Required correction

Define and enforce option-freeze and deadline guards transactionally in both stores, independently of authentication. Add regression cases for late ballots and post-opening/certification option writes.

## Verification

Memory and Postgres ballot writes recheck the opening window and deadline atomically. Options become immutable at opening, expired sessions cannot open, and lifecycle transitions have storage-level guards.

See [remediation](../../../testing/security/REMEDIATION.md) and
[final verification](../../../testing/security/remediation-validation.json) for
regression results, positive controls and deployment limits.
