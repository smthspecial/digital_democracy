## Finding

Severity: **Critical**. Assessment: **SEC-02**.

Anonymous audit events claiming voting-service were accepted and passed chain verification. An anonymous caller supplied two approval refs under one actor and released a caller-created protocol gate with past timestamps. The gate record changed; no external deployment was executed.

## Evidence

See [security report](../../../testing/security/REPORT.md) and its sanitized
memory/Postgres result files. Source locations, fixtures and scope limits are
recorded there. Run [TP-016](../../technical/test-plans/tp-016.md) for reproduction.

## Required correction

Derive provenance from authenticated producers; replace placeholder signatures where signatures assert authorship. Resolve authoritative approvals, verify independent eligible holders, and derive delay/visibility state.

## Verification

Audit writes authenticate and namespace their producer; HTTP signatures and NATS envelopes bind producer identity. Ed25519 replaces placeholder signatures. Protocol gates enforce a server-owned 24-hour delay and three current independent approval types from the restricted governance worker source. Direct caller approvals and malformed legacy gates are rejected.

See [remediation](../../../testing/security/REMEDIATION.md) and
[final verification](../../../testing/security/remediation-validation.json) for
regression results, positive controls and deployment limits.
