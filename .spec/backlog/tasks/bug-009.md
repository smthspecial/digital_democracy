## Finding

Severity: **High**. Assessment: **SEC-05**.

Anonymous duplicate scans returned IDs of two synthetic pending citizens. Registration burst queries were also public. Both use the broad worker connection; pairwise duplicate comparisons have quadratic cost. No exhaustion test was attempted.

## Evidence

See [security report](../../../testing/security/REPORT.md) and its sanitized
memory/Postgres result files. Source locations, fixtures and scope limits are
recorded there. Run [TP-016](../../technical/test-plans/tp-016.md) for reproduction.

## Required correction

Require authorized reviewer/workload context. Bound or schedule duplicate scans and avoid unnecessary pending-identity disclosure.

## Verification

Identity scans require signed api-go requests. Duplicate comparison rejects oversized synchronous populations and legacy handles; bounded signals prevent unbounded output. Larger populations require offline processing.

See [remediation](../../../testing/security/REMEDIATION.md) and
[final verification](../../../testing/security/remediation-validation.json) for
regression results, positive controls and deployment limits.
