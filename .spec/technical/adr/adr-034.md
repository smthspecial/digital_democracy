## Context

FR-007 requires multi-party approval for identity revocation/administration. `IamService.revoke` is unilateral by design (DP-072, "no dual control") with a passing test proving it. DP-035 lists three approval types (`citizen_supermajority`, `audit_confirmation`, `body_endorsement`) without saying how many of the three an identity-disable action needs. `GovernanceRoleType` has no `registrar` value. `ConflictOfInterest` is scoped to `ExpertDomain`, with no way to represent a conflict between an approver and the citizen being disabled.

## Decision

- **Approval requirement (D1)**: `IDENTITY_DISABLE_REQUIREMENT` = 2-of-2: `audit_confirmation` + `body_endorsement`, from distinct citizens and distinct role types. `citizen_supermajority` applies to protocol changes (FR-061/DP-043), not identity actions — DP-035 is amended to say so.
- **IamService.revoke scope (D2)**: split by target type. Citizen-scoped targets (identity disable) route through the new approval gate; non-citizen targets (policy/attachment revocation, DP-072's original case) stay unilateral by the audit role. FR-007's Description is amended: it governs actions removing a natural person's ability to participate, not all IAM revocation.
- **COI representation (D3)**: add nullable `relatedCitizenId` to `ConflictOfInterest`; the approval gate rejects an approver whose disclosed COI names the citizen being disabled.
- **Requester mapping (D4)**: US-004's "identity registrar" maps to the existing `operator` `GovernanceRoleType`. No new enum value — the approval gate, not the requester's title, carries FR-007's guarantee.
- **Verification evidence access (D5)**: `identity_verification.evidence_ref` is never returned by any API; an auditor can confirm a verification of a stated method occurred at a stated time without a pointer to the underlying document ever appearing in a response.
- **Anomalous registration detection (D6)**: flag >20 registrations/hour sharing no prior history, or handle-similarity (Levenshtein ≤2 after NFKC folding) among recent registrations. Never auto-suspend — flag for human review only.

## Consequences

E1-07 (schema), E1-08 (ApprovalGateService), E1-09 (revocation workflow), E1-11 (approver independence), E1-12 (route IamService.revoke through the gate), E1-13 (duplicate/anomaly detection) all build directly against this decision.
