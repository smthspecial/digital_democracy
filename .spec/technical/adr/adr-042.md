## Context

NFR-001 requires both unlinkable ballots and receipt-freeness for all elections.
Encrypting a ballot, verifying a range proof and threshold-decrypting a tally
do not by themselves satisfy either the anonymous credential or coercion claim.
No protocol has yet been selected or independently reviewed for this system.

## Proposed decision

Keep the existing production voting gate. Select a maintained reviewed protocol
only after an end-to-end prototype demonstrates every required election method,
anonymous eligibility, atomic credential spending, proof verification, threshold
decryption and reproducible public verification under explicit assumptions.
Do not replace the current placeholders with custom cryptographic primitives.

The candidate assessment is in
[the threat model](../../../testing/production-readiness/cryptography-assessment.md).
ElectionGuard and Belenios remain research candidates, not approved production
dependencies. Their component capabilities do not establish NFR-001 compliance.
An independent reviewer and independently administered trustees are prerequisites.

## Required design before acceptance

Pin protocol and implementation versions, license and review evidence. Bind the
election manifest, method, frozen options, public key and proof domain together.
Define issuer/submission separation, credential format and unlinkability,
threshold and collusion assumptions, public bulletin-board artifacts, tally
verification, complaint handling, backups and key destruction. Resolve the
receipt-freeness gap without silently weakening the requirements. Determine
whether revoting conflicts with the existing single-use credential requirement.

## Current result

Research, production configuration gates and a standard-parameter synthetic
component prototype are implemented. Five real proof/tally checks passed,
including 2-of-3 compensated decryption; anonymous credentials, the complete
remote-voting design, receipt-freeness and independent review remain outstanding.
Production opening, credential issuance, casting, tallying and certification
are explicitly blocked. This proposed ADR does not supersede ADR-002 or
authorize production voting.

## Local feasibility continuation — 2026-10-14

An [isolated RFC 9474 credential experiment](../../../testing/cryptography/README.md)
now tests blind issuance/unblinding, independent RSA-PSS verification, election
key/context binding and local duplicate-spend defenses using pinned CIRCL
v1.6.5. Its transferable bearer credentials, in-memory stores and single-process
fixture do not satisfy complete protocol or production acceptance. The
[assessment](../../../testing/production-readiness/cryptography-assessment.md)
adds BeleniosRF and the published critique of VoteAgain as research inputs.
This ADR remains proposed; no protocol is selected and the production gate
remains in force.
