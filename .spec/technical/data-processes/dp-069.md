## Description

Verifies the proposer holds an active `operator` or `platform_operator` governance role (live check against governance-role-service, never trusted from the request body). Creates an `access_policy` or `policy_attachment` row with `status: pending_approval`. Does not take effect until DP-070's dual-control endorsement activates it. Emits to `audit.append`.
