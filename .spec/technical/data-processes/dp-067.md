## Description

Deletes `session` rows (TBL-037) whose refresh token has expired and gone unused past a short retention grace window (24 hours past `expires_at`). Rows with `status=revoked` are never purged by this process — per TBL-037, they are retained indefinitely for audit and cannot be reactivated. Only naturally-expired, never-revoked session rows are removed, and only once stale enough to hold no forensic value.
