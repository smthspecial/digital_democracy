## Description

Records a verification attempt (national ID, passport, or government credential)
with its outcome supplied by a trusted verifier in an authenticated callback.
The citizen cannot submit their own outcome. When verified and currently
pending, identity-service activates the citizen, emits DP-036, and provisions
a limited first-passkey enrollment grant from auth-service. See ADR-040 for
callback authentication, retries, grant delivery and the passkey ceremony.
