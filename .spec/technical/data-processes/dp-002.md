## Description

Records a verification attempt (national ID, passport, or government credential) with its outcome (`verified` or `rejected`) supplied synchronously in the same call — there is no separate pending-review step. When the outcome is `verified` and the citizen's current status is `pending`, identity-service activates the citizen record and emits DP-036.
