## Responsibility

Owns flexible, policy-based access control for operator (AUTH-006) and platform-operator (AUTH-011) permissions only — citizen permissions (AUTH-001–005, 007, 008) stay governed by the fixed AUTH-010 catalog, unchanged, and service-to-service authorization (AUTH-012) stays governed by ADR-018's mesh mTLS policy, unchanged. Evaluates "may principal X take action Y on resource Z" for any service that calls it, and coordinates the dual-control grant / unilateral-revoke change flow (ADR-025, ARCH-024) so no single operator or platform-operator can grant themselves or anyone else new access.

## Key rules

- Granting (proposing a policy or an attachment) requires the proposer to hold an active `operator` or `platform_operator` governance role, verified live against governance-role-service; never trusted from the request body.
- A proposal activates only once a *different* citizen holding an active governance role of the *same* role_type as the proposer records an approved endorsement (dual control, ARCH-024 §2 — modeled on DP-068, not DP-035, since DP-035's three approval types have no mapping to the `implementation` layer these roles occupy).
- Revocation requires no dual control: any citizen holding an active `operator`, `platform_operator`, or `auditor` role may revoke a policy or attachment unilaterally and immediately (DP-068's own grant/revoke asymmetry — revoking is always easier than granting).
- Evaluation is default-deny, explicit-deny-overrides-allow (AWS IAM's algorithm) — an action with no matching active policy is denied, never implicitly permitted.
- Every policy and attachment is publicly readable — no hidden or exclusive grants (CON-005).
- Every propose/endorse/activate/revoke transition is emitted to `audit-service`; `evaluate` calls are not (read-path traffic, not a governance-relevant write).

## Dependencies

- Emits to: `audit-service` (DP-036)
- Reads from: `governance-role-service` (`GET /governance-roles/roles`) to verify proposer/endorser/revoker role_type and term liveness
- Called by: any service enforcing an AUTH-006/AUTH-011-gated operation (e.g. `budget-service`'s `ledger_entry:record`, `project-service`'s `milestone:report`, `governance-role-service`'s own `approval:submit:operator`) — none of these call sites are wired to it yet (ADR-025 §Consequences); this service itself has no callers until that follow-on wiring happens.
