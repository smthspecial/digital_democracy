## Description

The system shall scale to national populations and large volumes of concurrent proposals, votes, and reviews without degrading correctness or responsiveness.

## Metric

- Target: 10,000,000 registered citizens; 100,000 sustained concurrent active citizens, with burst headroom to 300,000 concurrent during national vote-close windows. Ballot-cast p99 latency <= 1 second at peak concurrency; read-heavy endpoints (proposal browsing, audit log) p99 <= 300-500ms; write endpoints p99 <= 800ms.
- Measurement: Load and chaos testing per the plan in ARCH-007 and ARCH-008, executed before every national vote session.

## Traceability

- Source: White Paper Goal 12; Module 4 (scalable governance); national thresholds (50,000+).
- Realizing specs: ADR-015, ADR-016, ADR-017, ARCH-006, ARCH-007.
