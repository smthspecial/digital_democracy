## Description

The system shall remain available and resilient, avoiding single points of control or failure in operation and oversight.

## Metric

- Target: 99.95% uptime per service (≤~4.4 hours downtime/year); multi-region active-active deployment across 3+ regions with automated failover; RTO ≤5 min and RPO ≤30 sec for governance-critical data (`ballot`, `audit_log`, `governance_role`); RTO ≤30 min and RPO ≤5 min otherwise.
- Measurement: Continuous uptime monitoring; quarterly full disaster-recovery drills with a real regional failover; results published to the audit log.

## Traceability

- Source: White Paper Risks 16 (platform control concentration); Module 6 (no single point of authority).
- Realized by: ADR-017; ARCH-008.
